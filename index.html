Install in 2 minutes

No installer, nothing to configure in Windows.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and about 1 GB of free space.
  • An internet connection and your Seafight account.
  • The computer must stay on: turn off sleep while the bot runs (Windows Settings → System → Power).
  1. Download the zip with the button above. Right-click it → Extract All → for example on your Desktop. Do not start it from inside the zip.
  2. Open the “Kraken Bot” folder and start Kraken Bot.exe. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Log in to Seafight in the window that opens, as you normally do in your browser, and press Play.
  4. The first time, a window shows your 2 free days and your computer ID (PC-XXXXXXXX). The ID is also always shown at the top left, under the name “Kraken Bot”.
  5. In the panel choose what the bot should do: NPC (which ships to sink), Chests, Items, Missions, Bonus maps. In Settings write your ship name.
  6. Press ▶ Start. To stop: F9 (from anywhere) or ESC (in the program).

While it runs: keep the Kraken Bot window open (do not minimise it) and do not click inside the game. Updates install themselves when you start the program.

FAQ

How does it play?

Like a player sitting at the computer: it clicks and presses keys in the game window. It does not change the game and does not send anything to the server by itself.

Does it need my password?

No. You log in yourself, in the program's own window. The bot never reads or stores your password.

Can I use my computer while it runs?

The game window needs to stay open. It works best when you leave it running on its own.

How do I update?

You don't have to: every time you start it, it checks for a new version and installs it by itself. Your settings stay as they are.

How does the licence work?

The licence is tied to your computer, not to your game account. Pay with your computer ID (shown in the panel) and the bot unlocks itself on that computer. If you change computers, write to us and we move the remaining time.

Where do I find my computer ID? Can it change?

It is at the top left of Kraken Bot, under the name (for example “ID PC-7F3A91C2 · trial until …”), and in the welcome window. It comes from Windows: it stays the same when you restart, update or reinstall Kraken Bot. It changes only if you reinstall Windows or use another computer — then write to us on Discord and we move your remaining time.

Does it need the internet for the licence?

It checks the licence when it starts and from time to time. Without internet it keeps working for 48 hours.
